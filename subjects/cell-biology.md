# Cell Biology

**Exam date:** 9 December 2026  
**NEP-2023 code:** BTBTC302 · **Archived CBCS code:** BTBTC06

> **Syllabus caution:** The following map comes from the archived NSUT CBCS Biotechnology scheme (2019–20). Match it with your current IMS/faculty syllabus before treating any topic as examinable.

## Topic map

- Cell theory, historical developments, microscopy, cell size/shape and architecture.
- **Organelles and biogenesis:** cell wall, nucleus, ER, Golgi, lysosomes, mitochondria, chloroplasts and microbodies.
- Cell cycle, mitosis, meiosis and regulation.
- Plasma membrane structure/function, carrier proteins, active transport, ion channels and pumps.
- **Cell signalling:** GPCRs, enzyme-linked receptors, ion/voltage-gated channels, second messengers and intracellular signalling.
- **Cytoskeleton:** microtubules, intermediate filaments, microfilaments and cell motility.
- **Apoptosis:** death domains, caspases, mitochondrial regulation and apoptosome; cancer, oncogenes and tumour suppressors.

## Self-test prompts — built from the topic map, not PYQs

1. Compare structure and functions of mitochondria, chloroplasts, ER, Golgi and lysosomes.
2. Explain simple diffusion, facilitated diffusion and active transport across membranes.
3. Draw the cell cycle and explain checkpoint roles; compare mitosis and meiosis.
4. Compare GPCR and enzyme-linked receptor signalling, including second messengers.
5. Compare microtubules, microfilaments and intermediate filaments by structure and function.
6. Describe the intrinsic apoptosis pathway, including mitochondrial regulation, caspases and apoptosome; relate it to cancer regulation.
