# Biochemistry

**Exam date:** 25 November 2026  
**NEP-2023 code:** BTBTC301 · **Archived CBCS code:** BTBTC04

> **Syllabus caution:** This topic map is a historical cross-check drawn from the archived NSUT CBCS Biotechnology scheme (2019–20), not a verified 2026 syllabus. Check every topic against current IMS/faculty material.

## Topic map

- **Bioenergetics:** first and second laws, free energy, chemical equilibrium, standard free-energy change (ΔG°), ATP–ADP cycle, mitochondria/chloroplasts and enzyme basics.
- **Carbohydrate metabolism:** glycolysis, glycogen synthesis and breakdown, TCA cycle, gluconeogenesis, glyoxylate pathway and pentose phosphate pathway.
- **Energy conversion:** electron transport chain, proton-motive force, ATP synthase, regulation, coupling/uncoupling, ETC inhibitors and photophosphorylation.
- **Other metabolism:** lipid, nucleic-acid and amino-acid metabolism.
- **Integration:** regulation of metabolism, metabolic disorders and recent advances.

- [ ] Draw glycolysis, TCA, PPP and ETC from memory.

- [ ] Explain pathway location, regulation and fed/fasting changes in flux.

- [ ] Compare glycolysis and gluconeogenesis.
- [ ] Practise ΔG, redox-potential and energy-coupling calculations from class examples.
- [ ] Write timed 5-, 8- and 10-mark answers with labelled diagrams.

## Suggested study method

Use your class notes first. Select one lecture or textbook section only when a specific gap remains, then close the resource and reproduce the pathway from memory. Finish with one class question or syllabus-matched PYQ.
