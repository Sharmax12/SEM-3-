Daily revision loop

  1. 10 minutes of closed-book recall from yesterday.
  2. One deep block for DSA or your weakest biology topic.
  3. One second-subject block; rotate Microbiology and Cell Biology.
  4. One timed problem set or answer-writing set.
  5. Update your mistake log and choose tomorrow’s first task.

Balance your revision

Rotate between four modes of preparation: problem-solving, structured concept answers, diagrams/pathways and numerical practice. Give extra time to topics where timed practice reveals gaps, and keep the plan aligned with the current faculty syllabus.

  • Practise at least one timed set for each subject.
  • Keep an error log and revisit recurring mistakes.
  • Use one primary source and one backup resource per topic.
