Course availability and current enrolment links can change; use the [NPTEL courses portal](https://nptel.ac.in/courses) to locate the relevant course. The study guide recommends using one primary source (class notes/faculty slides) and one backup resource per topic rather than attempting to finish entire MOOCs.

## Previous-year paper evidence

The study guide reports one publicly visible NSUT Biotechnology Semester III Microbiology mid-semester paper dated September 2023 (BTBTC08; 15 marks; 1.5 hours). The public listing filename contains “2024”, but the paper itself is dated September 2023. No complete, openly searchable NSUT ESE/PYQ set for all five subjects was verified at the time of research.

Ask your class representative, department office or library about official past-paper access. External university papers should be labelled as extra practice and matched to the current syllabus; never relabel them as NSUT PYQs.

## Research caveat

The archived NSUT 2019–20 scheme is used solely to build a provisional topic checklist. Newer schedules show NEP-2023 course-code mappings but do not establish that the detailed syllabus remains unchanged. Current IMS/faculty syllabus, lecture slides and announcements take precedence.
