# Curated video playlists and learning resources

This guide is designed around the five NSUT Semester III papers. **Use current IMS/faculty syllabus and class notes as the authority.** The archived NSUT CBCS topic map is only a historical checklist.

## How to use the video material

1. Start with the corresponding topic in your class notes.
2. Watch one lecture only for the gap you have identified.
3. Pause and reproduce the pathway, algorithm, workflow or numerical method without looking.
4. Solve a class question or syllabus-matched PYQ.
5. Record the exact mistake and revisit it later.

Do not attempt to complete every long playlist before the exams. The direct playlist links below are marked as such. Where a direct playlist URL could not be reliably established, the link opens YouTube's playlist-filtered search results or the official course page instead; it is not represented as a verified playlist URL.

## 1. Biochemistry — exam 25 November

### Best structured course
- [NPTEL: Biochemistry — IIT Madras](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/102106087), Prof. Subramaniam K. Its course outline includes biomolecules, enzymes, bioenergetics, glycolysis, TCA, oxidative phosphorylation, photosynthesis/carbon assimilation, nitrogen metabolism and lipid metabolism.

### YouTube playlists / video collections
- **[Biochemistry (New) — direct YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLYcLrRDaR8_dKo6Nu1Dw1efJGkDpuZGEy)** — a third-party-curated sequence described as 99 lectures across proteins, enzymes, carbohydrates, lipids and metabolism. It is not an official NSUT/NPTEL playlist.
- [Ninja Nerd metabolism collection](https://tutorialtube.online/playlist/biochemistry-3/) — a third-party index of 33 YouTube lectures (~15 hours), including glycolysis, ETC, glycogen metabolism, gluconeogenesis, lipid metabolism, amino-acid metabolism, urea cycle and PPP.
- [NPTEL YouTube Courses catalogue](https://nptel.ac.in/yt-courses) — check whether the NPTEL Biochemistry course is available as a structured YouTube Course.

### Additional resources
- [AK Lectures](https://aklectures.com/) — free lecture library with a large biochemistry section; useful for concept repair.
- [OpenStax Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/index) — supplementary material for cellular energetics/metabolism.
- [Metabolism Made Easy](https://medbiochem.org/) — additional explanations and audio/video material.

### Watch first
Bioenergetics and ΔG → glycolysis and regulation → gluconeogenesis/glycogen → TCA → ETC/ATP synthase → PPP and lipid/nitrogen metabolism as included in your syllabus.

## 2. Data Structures & Algorithms — exam 27 November

### Best structured course
- [NPTEL: Data Structures and Algorithms — IIT Delhi](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/106102064), Prof. Naveen Garg. The 36-lecture course covers stacks, queues, linked lists, hashing, trees, AVL/red-black trees, heaps, sorting, graphs, BFS/DFS, spanning trees and shortest paths.
- [Class Central: NPTEL DSA course](https://www.classcentral.com/course/youtube-computer-sc-data-structures-and-algorithms-47663) — free video-course listing with the complete lecture outline and YouTube as provider.

### YouTube playlist / video options
- [Abdul Bari — Algorithms (direct YouTube playlist)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLDN4rrl48XKpZkf03iYFl-O29szjTrs_O) — algorithms, design and analysis.
- [MyCodeSchool — Data Structures (direct YouTube playlist)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL2_aWCzGMAwI3W_JlcBbtYTwiQSsOTa6P) — core data structures and implementation concepts.
- [NPTEL IIT Delhi DSA — YouTube playlist search](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=NPTEL+Data+Structures+and+Algorithms+IIT+Delhi+Naveen+Garg+playlist&sp=EgIQAw%3D%3D). Prefer the nptelhrd / NPTEL series when choosing among results.
- [Bro Code: Data Structures and Algorithms Full Course](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CBYHwZcbD-s) — one long video (not a playlist), useful as a quick visual recap; it uses Java.
- [Dinesh Varyani: DSA with visualisations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iCHf7OZn6c) — supplementary visual explanation with a linked topic playlist in the video description; Java is used for demonstrations.

### Interactive resources
- [OpenDSA](https://opendsa.org/) — open interactive eTextbook modules, algorithm visualisations and self-assessment.
- [VisuAlgo](https://visualgo.net/en) — step-by-step visualisations for lists, trees, heaps, graphs and sorting.
- [MIT OpenCourseWare 6.006](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-006-introduction-to-algorithms-fall-2011/) — lecture notes, assignments and exams; use only the parts matching the NSUT syllabus.
- [GeeksforGeeks DSA](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/data-structures/) — secondary explanations and examples.

### Watch/practise first
Arrays/complexity → linked lists → stacks/queues → tree construction/traversals → AVL and red-black trees → hashing/heaps → searching/sorting → graph representation and BFS/DFS. Every lecture must be followed by a hand-traced example.

## 3. Chemical Engineering Principles — exam 3 December

### Best structured course
- [NPTEL: Basic Principles and Calculations in Chemical Engineering — IIT Guwahati](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/103103165), Prof. Subrata Kumar Majumder. Topics include units/dimensions, process variables, material balances, recycle/bypass, reactive and combustion balances, phase equilibrium, psychrometrics and energy balances.

### YouTube playlists / video collections
- **[Chemical Process Calculations — direct YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL23LJMmRTn8fwtijrPEgIbqZAaKCc3oEg)** — titled “Chemical Process Calculations Lecture series”; it is cited as an online resource in a Chemical Engineering department syllabus. Treat it as supplementary, not NSUT-specific.
- [Chemical Engineering Calculations — 15-video collection](https://tutorialtube.online/playlist/chemical-engineering-calculations/) — third-party index (~14 hours) with lectures on process variables, material balances, multiple units, recycle/bypass/purge, conversion/yield and combustion calculations.
- [NPTEL YouTube Courses catalogue](https://nptel.ac.in/yt-courses) — search for the course title to check for the structured YouTube version.

### Practice and worked examples
- [LearnChemE: Material and Energy Balances](https://learncheme.com/course-overview/material-and-energy-balances-resources/) — 225+ screencasts, interactive self-study modules and simulations are listed on the course resource page.
- [LearnChemE: Material Balance self-study module](https://learncheme.com/quiz-yourself/interactive-self-study-modules/material-balances/material-balances-introduction/) — balance equation, example workflow, simulations and self-testing.
- [LearnChemE: Kinetics/Reactor Design](https://learncheme.com/screencasts/kinetics-reactor-design/) — batch, CSTR and PFR worked topics.
- [LearnChemE: Process Control](https://learncheme.com/screencasts/process-control/) — control theory and feedback-loop topics.
- [VTU Online Class: Basic Principles and Calculations in Chemical Engineering](https://online.vtu.ac.in/v1/course-details/Basic-Principles-and-Calculations-in-Chemical-Engineering) — another structured lecture sequence with previews.

### Watch/practise first
Units and basis → simple material balances → recycle/bypass/purge → reactive/combustion balances → energy balances → batch/CSTR/PFR equations → feedback-control loop. Solve numericals before watching the solutions.

## 4. Microbiology — exam 5 December

### Best structured course
- [NPTEL: Microbiology — IIT Guwahati](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/102103015), Dr. Gurvinder Kaur Saini. It covers history, cell structure, microscopy/staining, nutrition and pure-culture isolation, microbial growth/control, metabolism, genetics, diversity, viruses and microbial disease.
- [NPTEL syllabus PDF](https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/syllabus_pdf/102103015.pdf) — structured topic checklist and course outline.

### YouTube playlist / video options
- [NPTEL IIT Guwahati Microbiology — YouTube playlist search](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=NPTEL+Microbiology+IIT+Guwahati+Gurvinder+Kaur+Saini+playlist&sp=EgIQAw%3D%3D). Prefer the official NPTEL/nptelhrd series when available.
- [General Microbiology Lectures — Dr. Julie Wells (15-video lecture series)](https://tutorialtube.online/topic/biology-microbiology/) — third-party index of an Orange Coast College lecture series (~32.5 hours) with introductory microbiology, chemistry/macromolecules and prokaryotic/eukaryotic cells.
- [Microbiology — KCL Tutorials (35-video collection)](https://tutorialtube.online/playlist/microbiology-3/) — third-party index covering history, classification, bacterial structure and related B.Pharm topics. Use selectively.
- [Pearson: Microbial growth, nutrition and bacterial cell structure](https://www.pearson.com/channels/microbiology/study-guides/microbial-growth-nutrition-and-bacterial-cell-structure/video-lessons) — short topic-specific clips.
- [Pearson: Culture media and microbial growth](https://www.pearson.com/channels/microbiology/study-guides/culture-media-and-quantification-of-microbial-growth/video-lessons) — selected clips on growth measurement and cultivation.

### Free textbook and support
- [OpenStax Microbiology](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/index) — free textbook for microbial structure, growth, control, metabolism and diversity.
- [NSUT library e-resources](https://idp.nsut.ac.in/) — check authorised access to additional books and journals.

### Watch first
History/germ theory → prokaryotic structure → microscopy/staining → media and pure culture → microbial growth curve → sterilisation/control → microbial diversity → metabolism. For methods, write purpose, principle, steps, controls/confirmation and result.

## 5. Cell Biology — exam 9 December

### Best structured courses
- [NPTEL: Cell Biology — IIT Guwahati](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/102103012), Dr. Sanjukta Patra. Covers organelles, membrane organisation/transport, cytoskeleton, cell cycle, mitosis/meiosis and cell signalling.
- [NPTEL: Molecular Cell Biology — IIT Madras](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/102106025), Prof. Devarajan Karunagaran. More advanced; covers signalling pathways, apoptosis, cancer biology and infection. Use selected lectures only.

### YouTube playlist / video options
- [NPTEL IIT Guwahati Cell Biology — YouTube playlist search](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=NPTEL+Cell+Biology+IIT+Guwahati+Sanjukta+Patra+playlist&sp=EgIQAw%3D%3D). Prefer the NPTEL series when available.
- [Ninja Nerd: Cell Structure and Function](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t5DvF5OVr1Y) — detailed lecture on organelles, membranes and cytoskeleton.
- [Ninja Nerd: Cell Cycle — Interphase and Mitosis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUDws4JrIiI) — focused lecture on cell-cycle phases and mitosis.
- [Ninja Nerd Cell Biology — 18-video collection](https://tutorialtube.online/topic/biology-cell-biology/) — third-party index of a ~13.5-hour set covering cell cycle, regulation, membrane transport and organelle-linked disorders.
- [Ninja Nerd Cell Biology topic index](https://www.ninjanerd.org/topic/cell-biology) — grouped topic lessons on cell cycle, organelles, transport, cytoskeleton and cellular disorders.
- [Molecular & Cellular Biology — UNF Spring 2021 (50-video lecture collection)](https://tutorialtube.online/topic/biology-cell-biology/) — optional, university-style reference (~40.9 hours).

### Free textbook and interactive resources
- [OpenStax Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/index) — chapters on eukaryotic cells, membranes, cell communication and division.
- [OpenStax: The Cell Cycle](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/10-2-the-cell-cycle) — interphase, mitosis, cytokinesis and G0.
- [HHMI BioInteractive: Cell Division Is Essential to Life](https://media.hhmi.org/biointeractive/click/cellcycle/) — interactive on cell-cycle phases, checkpoints, regulators and cancer.
- [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/) — videos and interactives across cell biology, microbiology and biochemistry/molecular biology.

### Watch first
Organelles → membrane transport → cytoskeleton → cell cycle/checkpoints → mitosis/meiosis → GPCR and enzyme-linked signalling → intrinsic apoptosis. Draw every mechanism from memory after watching.

## Important source and syllabus caveats

- NPTEL pages are structured course references, not proof that every topic is in your NSUT 2026 syllabus.
- The direct Biochemistry playlist above is curated by a third party, not by NPTEL or NSUT.
- The direct Chemical Process Calculations playlist is general Chemical Engineering practice, not an NSUT playlist.
- The YouTube playlist-search links are search pages filtered to playlist results; verify uploader and course coverage before starting.
- Do not spend time on optional advanced topics unless they appear in your faculty syllabus.
- Use only resources you can access lawfully; do not redistribute paywalled or restricted course materials.
